Measuring tool-use reliability in long tasks

Our evaluation follows agents across multi-step tasks rather than scoring a single response. Each trial records tool selection, argument validity, observable side effects, and final task completion.

Across the frozen test set, the largest source of failure was recovery after a transient tool error. The accompanying repository contains the prompts, deterministic scorer, and per-example outcomes.
